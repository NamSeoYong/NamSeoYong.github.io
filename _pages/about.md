I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Applied Artificial Intelligence at **Hanyang University**, where I work in the **Multimodal Intelligence and Interaction Laboratory (MILab)** under the guidance of Prof. **Yongjae Yoo**.

My research sits at the intersection of **haptics, multimodal AI, and computer vision**. I build systems that automatically generate and render multisensory experiences — 4D effects, vibrotactile and thermal feedback, and directly touchable representations of visual scenes — to make immersive media richer and more accessible. Recent work includes AI-assisted authoring tools for haptic content (HAPhy), direct haptic rendering of Gaussian Splatting scenes, and audio–tactile accessibility systems for visually impaired users.

I am increasingly interested in bridging these perceptual and generative systems with **robot AI**, including vision–language–action models and video world models for real-world manipulation.
