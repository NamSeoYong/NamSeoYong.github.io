Interpreting a **Gaussian Splatting** scene as a dense point cloud so a user can directly press, feel, and deform photorealistic captures. Vision and touch share a single analytic dent field, keeping deformation consistent within a 1 kHz haptic budget, with a time-domain passivity controller ensuring stable interaction.

- **Period:** 2025 – present · Funded by NRF (RS-2023-00212914)
