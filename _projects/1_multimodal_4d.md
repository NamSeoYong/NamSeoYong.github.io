A pipeline that analyzes the audio and visual content of a video and automatically authors multisensory **4D effects** (vibration, heat, wind) that align with on-screen events, so immersive media can be produced without manual per-scene effect design.

- **Lab:** MILab, Hanyang University · **Role:** Lead
- **Period:** 2023 – present
- **Published at:** SIGGRAPH Asia 2024 Technical Communications (Oral); ACM Multimedia 2026 (TaME4D)
